Privacy

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, email, and for tester applications your state, phone type, the games you play, your answer to "why", and an optional Facebook name.

How we use it

To send Tic Tap Due tester and launch emails, to pick testers, and (if you checked the box) Number State news. We don't sell your information.

Where it's kept

Form entries are stored by our website host, Netlify, and read only by the Tic Tap Due owners.

Unsubscribe or delete

Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to tiptapdue@gmail.com and we'll remove your information.
